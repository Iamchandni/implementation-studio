# Personal Loan — Sample Business Requirements

Document ID: DOC-PL-001
Version: 1.0
Prepared for: Prototype demonstration

## 1. Applicant eligibility

Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years of age, inclusive.

Applicants must have a minimum monthly salary of INR 30,000.

The applicant's salary must have been credited to the bank for at least six consecutive months.

## 2. Loan amount

The minimum personal loan amount is INR 50,000.

The maximum personal loan amount is INR 25,00,000.

## 3. Approval workflow

Loan applications with an amount greater than INR 10,00,000 require approval from two levels: a Manager followed by a Senior Manager.

Applications with an amount of INR 10,00,000 or less require approval from one Manager.

High-risk applicants should undergo additional verification.

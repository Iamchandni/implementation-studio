# Retail Account Opening — Business Requirements

- The application must capture full name, date of birth, mobile number and email address.
- Applicants must be at least 18 years old.
- The system must verify the applicant mobile number using a one-time password.
- The applicant must upload one accepted address proof document.
- Applications flagged by the risk engine must be routed to manual review.
- Application records must be retained for the legally approved retention period.

The retention period and governing policy are intentionally unspecified and must be clarified with the business owner.
